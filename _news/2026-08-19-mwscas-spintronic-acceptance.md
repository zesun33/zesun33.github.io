🎉 Thrilled to share that our paper **"Leveraging Intrinsic Physics of Spintronic Devices for Neuromorphic Computing"** has been accepted as an **invited special session paper** at **MWSCAS 2026**!

We highlight how magnetic tunnel junctions can leverage intrinsic spintronic phenomena—STT, SOT, VCMA, magnetoelectric effects, and stochastic superparamagnetic dynamics—as native computational primitives for neuromorphic hardware, with system-level examples spanning selectorless synaptic crossbars and sparse superparamagnetic neuronal encoding.

**Authors**: Md Zesun Ahmed Mia, Abhronil Sengupta
